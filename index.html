System FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about duration parsing, playback math, and privacy.

🔒 Is YouTube account login required? Privacy-First

No. PlaylistPilot requires zero authentication or accounts. Simply paste any public YouTube playlist URL and build your schedule instantly.

🧩 How does long video splitting work? Context Protection

If a single video exceeds your remaining daily quota, PlaylistPilot intelligently splits it across adjacent days, adding a 3-minute overlap buffer on the continuation segment to ensure seamless comprehension.

💾 Where is progress saved? Browser Storage

All checked items, speed configurations, and target dates are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage. Your data remains 100% private and persistent across visits.
